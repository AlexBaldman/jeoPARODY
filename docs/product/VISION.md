# Product Vision

JeoPARODY is a comedic trivia and learning product presented as a programmable game-show world.

The immediate product must remain fun, legible, fast, and genuinely educational. The long-range architectural opportunity is that its Stage, performance, asset, and semantic-event patterns may become reusable across other worlds **after JeoPARODY proves them**.

## Product promise

- trivia that feels like entertainment rather than coursework;
- humor woven into host performance, UI, camera/stage behavior, and environmental gags without burying gameplay;
- strong learning loops and content depth beneath the comedy;
- expressive hosts, themes, modes, sets, media, and future multiplayer surfaces;
- deterministic gameplay and accessible presentation even as the show becomes stranger.

## Architectural hypothesis

```text
DOMAIN / WORLD TRUTH
        ↓
SEMANTIC EVENTS
        ↓
EXPERIENCE / PERFORMANCE DIRECTION
        ↓
STAGE / PROJECTION
```

JeoPARODY is the place where this hypothesis earns reality. Broader projects may later reuse the proven grammar with different projections such as notebook, 2D world, 3D world, audio, illustrated story, or portfolio.

## Comedy doctrine

Comedy is a system of bounded channels, not a command to make every pixel shout.

Potential channels include host performance, content/clue writing, physical/environmental gags, camera/presentation, UI behavior, audio, and recurring lore. Directors should control density, timing, callbacks, and escalation so gameplay retains a readable center.

## Platform pressure tests

The ICM preserves broader projects and worlds such as uINVERSE, You in Verse, Brazillionaire, Memory Universe, Archimedes, Zeke Discovers, Stool Samples, ALgoRHYTHM B, and the Asset Library/Sprite Foundry.

They are valuable because they test portability of semantic identity, Stage, performance, asset provenance, and tooling. They do not authorize a premature universal-engine rewrite.

## The Russian-doll principle

Whenever repeated creation work reveals a stable reusable tool, consider turning that operation into infrastructure. The project should increasingly build **the system that builds the system** while resisting ornamental abstraction that has not earned a real consumer.
